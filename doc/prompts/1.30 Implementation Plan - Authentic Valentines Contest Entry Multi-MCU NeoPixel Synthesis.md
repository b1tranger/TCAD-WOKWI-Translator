# Authentic Valentine's Day Contest Entry (Multi-MCU & NeoPixel Heart)

## User Review Required

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - By inspecting the authentic circuit preview rendered from Tinkercad (`t725.png` for `lVMT4Ph4fKI-valentines-day-contest-entry`), we discovered that HLModtech's award-winning design is not a single-Arduino circuit with 3 standard LEDs.
> - The real circuit is an elaborate **Multi-Microcontroller NeoPixel Heart System**:
>   - **4 Arduino Uno R3** microcontrollers (1 Master MCU + 3 distributed Slave MCUs)
>   - **6 NeoPixel LED strips** configured geometrically to form a heart outline
>   - **3 NeoPixel Rings/Jewels** positioned at the heart's outer vertices and bottom tip
>   - **16x2 I2C LCD Display** positioned in the center of the heart
>   - **Half Breadboard** at the bottom with 10k potentiometer brightness dimmer, animation cycle pushbutton, and pull-down resistors
>   - **5V Power Supply Module** at top left providing clean power rails across the system
>   - **Intricate Inter-MCU Wiring Network** syncing animation states and data lines between all 4 Arduinos, the NeoPixel heart array, and the central LCD.
