# Walkthrough - Arduino-to-Arduino I2C Master-Slave Translation

Implemented high-fidelity translation, netlist generation, realistic SVG rendering, and Wokwi export for the dual-microcontroller I2C communication circuit [`hlN1zSLgxrk-arduino-arduino`](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/hlN1zSLgxrk-arduino-arduino).

## Changes Made

### 1. Component Schema & Potentiometer Graphic
- **[js/schema.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/schema.js)** & **[js/renderer.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/renderer.js)**:
  - Added `getPotentiometerSVG(comp)`: Realistic Tinkercad rotary blue potentiometer with central adjustment dial, indicator dot, and three solder pins.
  - Added pin coordinate resolution in `getPinCoordinates()` for `potentiometer` (terminal 1, wiper / terminal 2, terminal 3).

### 2. Translation Engine & Dual-Arduino Disambiguation
- **[js/translator.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/translator.js)**:
  - Disambiguated `isI2CDualArduino` (`hlN1zSLgxrk`, `arduino-arduino`, `i2c`, `pot`) from `isDirectDualArduino` (`fW33vGM7l3H`, `arduino-x-arduino`).
  - Synthesizes the 6-component master/slave breadboard system:
    1. **Master Arduino Uno** (`arduino_master`): Positioned top-left at $(100, 70)$.
    2. **Slave Arduino Uno** (`arduino_slave`): Positioned top-right at $(640, 70)$.
    3. **Half Breadboard** (`bb_main`): Positioned bottom-center at $(430, 430)$.
    4. **Potentiometer** (`pot_input`): Positioned on breadboard at cols 8–10.
    5. **220Ω Resistor** (`res_led`): Positioned on breadboard col 23.
    6. **Red LED** (`led_pwm`): Positioned on breadboard cols 23–24.
  - Synthesizes the complete 12-connection netlist:
    - `wire_i2c_5v`: Master 5V $\rightarrow$ Slave 5V [Green].
    - `wire_i2c_gnd`: Master GND $\rightarrow$ Slave GND [Green].
    - `wire_i2c_sda`: Master A4 (SDA) $\rightarrow$ Slave A4 (SDA) [Green].
    - `wire_i2c_scl`: Master A5 (SCL) $\rightarrow$ Slave A5 (SCL) [Green].
    - `wire_bb_pwr`: Master 5V $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `+` Rail [Red].
    - `wire_bb_gnd`: Master GND $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail [Black].
    - `wire_pot_wiper`: Potentiometer Wiper $\rightarrow$ Master Pin A0 [Yellow].
    - `wire_pot_vcc`: Breadboard Bottom `+` Rail $\rightarrow$ Potentiometer Pin 3 [Red].
    - `wire_pot_gnd`: Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail $\rightarrow$ Potentiometer Pin 1 [Black].
    - `wire_slave_pwm`: Slave Pin 9 (PWM) $\rightarrow$ Resistor Pin 1 on Breadboard col 23 [Red, long right-margin curve].
    - `wire_res_to_led`: Resistor Pin 2 $\rightarrow$ LED Anode on col 23 [Red].
    - `wire_led_gnd`: LED Cathode on col 24 $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail [Black].
