# Implementation Plan - Arduino-to-Arduino I2C Master-Slave Translation

Accurately translate and render the Autodesk Tinkercad I2C communication circuit [`hlN1zSLgxrk-arduino-arduino`](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/hlN1zSLgxrk-arduino-arduino) featuring two Arduino Uno boards communicating over an I2C bus, a half breadboard with a potentiometer analog input to the Master Arduino (Pin A0), and a PWM dimmable LED on the Slave Arduino (Pin 9).

## User Review Required

> [!IMPORTANT]
> While both `fW33vGM7l3H` and `hlN1zSLgxrk` share "arduino-arduino" in their names, `hlN1zSLgxrk` is a distinct **I2C Master-Writer / Slave-Receiver system** with a half breadboard, analog potentiometer, 4-wire I2C bus (5V, GND, A4 SDA, A5 SCL), and PWM LED output on Pin 9. We will provide dedicated disambiguation so each Tinkercad project translates to its exact visual schematic and netlist.

## Proposed Changes

### Translator Pipeline

#### [MODIFY] [js/translator.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/translator.js)
- Disambiguate the two dual-Arduino configurations:
  - **`isI2CDualArduino`** (matches `hln1zsl` or `i2c` or `pot`):
    - **Master Arduino Uno** (`arduino_master` / `arduino_tx`): Positioned top-left at $(120, 100)$.
    - **Slave Arduino Uno** (`arduino_slave` / `arduino_rx`): Positioned top-right at $(640, 100)$.
    - **Half Breadboard** (`bb_main`): Positioned bottom-center at $(460, 420)$.
    - **Potentiometer** (`pot_input`): Positioned on breadboard at cols 8–10, with wiper output to Master Arduino A0.
    - **220Ω Resistor** (`res_led`): Positioned on breadboard col 23.
    - **Red LED** (`led_pwm`): Positioned on breadboard cols 23–24.
  - Complete 10-wire netlist:
    1. `wire_i2c_5v`: Master 5V $\rightarrow$ Slave 5V [Green].
    2. `wire_i2c_gnd`: Master GND $\rightarrow$ Slave GND [Green].
    3. `wire_i2c_sda`: Master A4 (SDA) $\rightarrow$ Slave A4 (SDA) [Green].
    4. `wire_i2c_scl`: Master A5 (SCL) $\rightarrow$ Slave A5 (SCL) [Green].
    5. `wire_bb_pwr`: Master 5V $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `+` Rail [Red].
    6. `wire_bb_gnd`: Master GND $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail [Black].
    7. `wire_pot_wiper`: Potentiometer Wiper $\rightarrow$ Master Pin A0 [Yellow].
    8. `wire_pot_vcc`: Breadboard Bottom `+` Rail $\rightarrow$ Potentiometer Pin 3 [Red].
    9. `wire_pot_gnd`: Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail $\rightarrow$ Potentiometer Pin 1 [Black].
    10. `wire_slave_pwm`: Slave Pin 9 (PWM) $\rightarrow$ Resistor Pin 1 on Breadboard (col 23) [Red, looping around right margin].
    11. `wire_res_to_led`: Resistor Pin 2 $\rightarrow$ LED Anode (col 23) [Direct on board].
    12. `wire_led_gnd`: LED Cathode (col 24) $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail [Black].
  - **`isDirectDualArduino`** (matches `fw33vgm` or pushbutton serial link):
    - Retains the pushbutton + pull-down + multimeter configuration from Turn 10.
