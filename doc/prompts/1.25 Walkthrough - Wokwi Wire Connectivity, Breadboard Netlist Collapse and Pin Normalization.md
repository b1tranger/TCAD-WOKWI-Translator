# Walkthrough - Wokwi Wire Connectivity, Breadboard Netlist Collapse and Pin Normalization

## Overview & Problem Statement
When importing or translating Tinkercad circuits, all wires displayed cleanly on the web application's interactive SVG canvas, but opening or simulating the generated `diagram.json` in Wokwi resulted in missing wires and an empty circuit simulation.

### Root Cause Analysis
1. **Wokwi Simulator Has No Breadboard Element**: Wokwi's simulator engine only renders active electronic components (`wokwi-led`, `wokwi-resistor`, `wokwi-arduino-uno`, etc.) and does not support breadboard carrier parts (`wokwi-breadboard-*`). Previously, `toWokwi` exported breadboard parts (`bb_main`) and routed wires to them (`bb_main:bottom_rail_pos_1`, `bb_main:tie_row_15`), causing Wokwi to reject the part and drop all connections touching it.
2. **Case-Sensitive & Specific Pin Requirements**: Wokwi pin names are strictly validated:
   - `wokwi-led` requires uppercase `A` (anode) and `C` (cathode). Our code emitted lowercase `a` / `c`, which Wokwi rejected.
   - `wokwi-resistor` requires pins `1` and `2`.
   - `wokwi-arduino-uno` ground requires `GND.1`.
3. **Missing Breadboard Netlist Collapse**: Physical breadboards share electrical continuity along tie strips and power rails. In Wokwi, because there is no breadboard, those shared nodes must be collapsed into direct component-to-component connection tuples (`[ "partA:pin", "partB:pin", "color" ]`).

## Changes Made

### 1. Pin Name Normalization Engine (`js/translator.js`)
Implemented `normalizeWokwiPin(wokwiType, rawPin)` to map any raw Tinkercad, native, or informal pin identifier into Wokwi's exact case-sensitive specification:
- `wokwi-led`: `anode` / `a` $\rightarrow$ `A`, `cathode` / `c` $\rightarrow$ `C`.
- `wokwi-resistor`: `terminal1` / `t1` $\rightarrow$ `1`, `terminal2` / `t2` $\rightarrow$ `2`.
- `wokwi-pushbutton`: `terminal1a` $\rightarrow$ `1.l`, `terminal2a` $\rightarrow$ `2.l`, `terminal1b` $\rightarrow$ `1.r`, `terminal2b` $\rightarrow$ `2.r`.
- `wokwi-potentiometer`: `terminal1` $\rightarrow$ `GND`, `wiper` $\rightarrow$ `SIG`, `terminal2` $\rightarrow$ `VCC`.
- `wokwi-hc-sr04`: `vcc` $\rightarrow$ `VCC`, `trig` $\rightarrow$ `TRIG`, `echo` $\rightarrow$ `ECHO`, `gnd` $\rightarrow$ `GND`.
- `wokwi-servo`: `gnd` $\rightarrow$ `GND`, `vcc` / `pwr` $\rightarrow$ `V+`, `sig` / `pwm` $\rightarrow$ `PWM`.
- `wokwi-buzzer`: `pos` / `+` $\rightarrow$ `1`, `neg` / `-` $\rightarrow$ `2`.
- `wokwi-photoresistor-sensor`: `vcc` $\rightarrow$ `VCC`, `gnd` $\rightarrow$ `GND`, `d0` $\rightarrow$ `DO`, `a0` $\rightarrow$ `AO`.
- `wokwi-arduino-uno` / `nano` / `mega`: `gnd` $\rightarrow$ `GND.1`, digital `0`..`13`, analog `A0`..`A5`, `5v` $\rightarrow$ `5V`, `vin` $\rightarrow$ `VIN`.

### 2. Breadboard Netlist Collapse & Omission (`js/translator.js`)
Refactored `Translator.toWokwi(project)`:
- **Breadboard Parts Excluded**: Filtered out breadboards (`breadboard-half`, `breadboard-full`, `breadboard-mini`) from Wokwi `parts` so Wokwi receives only valid, simulatable physical components.
- **Electrical Net Grouping**:
  - Traverses all wires and resolves breadboard tie points into discrete electrical nets:
    - `PWR_TOP_POS` (top $+$ rail)
    - `PWR_TOP_NEG` (top $-$ rail)
    - `PWR_BTM_POS` (bottom $+$ rail)
    - `PWR_BTM_NEG` (bottom $-$ rail)
    - Tie columns (grouping upper/lower tie strips sharing electrical continuity)
  - Inspects seated component pin coordinates to detect implicit terminal-to-rail and terminal-to-column connections.
- **Direct Wire Generation**:
  - Synthesizes direct wire pairs connecting physical source pins (e.g. Arduino GND or MCU GPIO) to seated component terminals (e.g. Resistor pin 1, LED cathode pin C).
  - Short-circuit protection: guarantees `target.compId !== hub.compId` to eliminate invalid internal self-connections.
  - Wire coloring: assigns standard electrical colors (`black` for GND rails, `red` for positive rails, and signal colors for data lines).

### 3. Circuit Cathode-to-Resistor Ingestion Fixes (`js/translator.js`)
Updated the Traffic Light detector and universal circuit synthesizer to emit explicit LED cathode-to-resistor ties (`w_lr_res`, `w_ly_res`, `w_lg_res`), ensuring physical breadboard bridging is preserved and synthesized cleanly.
