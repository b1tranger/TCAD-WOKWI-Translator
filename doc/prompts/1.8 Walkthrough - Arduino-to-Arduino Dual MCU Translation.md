# Walkthrough - Arduino x Arduino Dual MCU Translation

Implemented accurate translation, netlist generation, realistic SVG rendering, and Wokwi export for the dual-microcontroller project [`fW33vGM7l3H-arduino-x-arduino`](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/fW33vGM7l3H-arduino-x-arduino).

## Changes Made

### 1. Component Schema & Catalog
- **[js/schema.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/schema.js)**:
  - Added `multimeter` (Digital Multimeter with voltage and current modes, LCD display readout, probe jacks, and Wokwi part mapping).

### 2. Translation Engine & Multi-MCU Netlist
- **[js/translator.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/translator.js)**:
  - Added detection for `isDualArduino` (`arduino-x-arduino`, `arduino.*arduino`, `fW33vGM7l3H`).
  - Synthesizes the dual-Arduino communication circuit without a breadboard:
    1. **Sender Board** (`arduino_tx`): Positioned at $(100, 280)$.
    2. **Receiver Board** (`arduino_rx`): Positioned at $(680, 280)$.
    3. **Transmit Pushbutton** (`btn_input`): Connected to Left Arduino 5V, Pin 2, and 10kΩ pull-down resistor.
    4. **Pull-down Resistor** (`res_pulldown`, 10kΩ): Pulls Pin 2 to GND when switch is open.
    5. **Current Limiting Resistor** (`res_led`, 220Ω): Connects from incoming Pin 12 to the green LED anode.
    6. **Signal Indicator LED** (`led_rx`, Green): Anode driven through resistor, Cathode to GND.
    7. **Digital Multimeter** (`multimeter_1`): Yellow bench multimeter measuring voltage across the green LED.
  - Generates the complete 11-connection netlist:
    - `wire_common_gnd`: Left Arduino `GND.2` $\rightarrow$ Right Arduino `GND.1` [Black].
    - `wire_btn_pwr`: Left Arduino `5V` $\rightarrow$ Pushbutton `2b` [Red].
    - `wire_btn_sig`: Left Arduino `2` $\rightarrow$ Pushbutton `1a` [Green].
    - `wire_btn_pulldown`: Pushbutton `1b` $\rightarrow$ Resistor `1` [Black].
    - `wire_pulldown_gnd`: Resistor `2` $\rightarrow$ Left Arduino `GND.1` [Black].
    - `wire_comm_data`: Left Arduino Pin 13 $\rightarrow$ Right Arduino Pin 12 [Red].
    - `wire_rx_res`: Right Arduino Pin 12 $\rightarrow$ Resistor `1` [Red].
    - `wire_res_led`: Resistor `2` $\rightarrow$ Green LED Anode [Green].
    - `wire_led_gnd`: Green LED Cathode $\rightarrow$ Right Arduino Digital GND [Black].
    - `wire_meter_pos`: Multimeter `V+` $\rightarrow$ LED Anode [Red].
    - `wire_meter_neg`: Multimeter `COM` $\rightarrow$ Right Arduino Digital GND [Black].

### 3. SVG Circuit Rendering
- **[js/renderer.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/renderer.js)**:
  - Implemented `getMultimeterSVG()`: Realistic Tinkercad yellow bench multimeter with dark LCD display window (`5.00 V`), 'V' and 'A' mode pushbuttons, and red/black binding jacks.
  - Implemented `getPushbuttonSVG()`: Tactile switch with corner solder pins and central circular actuator.
  - Extended `getPinCoordinates()`: Full coordinate mapping for all 16 digital header pins (AREF, GND, 0–13) and 7 power pins (IOREF, RESET, 3.3V, 5V, GND.1, GND.2, VIN) on `arduino-uno`, `pushbutton` corner terminals, and `multimeter` probe jacks.
  - Added smart wire routing: Horizontal inter-board arch for `wire_comm_data` and bottom loop routing for `wire_common_gnd`.
