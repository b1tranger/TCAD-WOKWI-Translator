# Walkthrough - Under-Voltage Protection System Translation

Implemented high-fidelity translation, netlist synthesis, realistic SVG rendering, and Wokwi export for the Autodesk Tinkercad project [`3q5bLzaJGOu-under-voltage-protection-system-`](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/3q5bLzaJGOu-under-voltage-protection-system-).

## Changes Made

### 1. Component Schema & Catalog
- **[js/schema.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/schema.js)**:
  - Registered `battery-9v` (`wokwi-battery-9v`) with 9V rating and snap connectors.
  - Registered `buzzer` (`wokwi-buzzer`) with positive and negative terminals.
  - Registered `lcd1602-i2c` (`wokwi-lcd1602`) with I2C backpack interface.

### 2. Translation Engine & Netlist Synthesis
- **[js/translator.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/translator.js)**:
  - Added detection for `isUnderVoltage` (`3q5blz`, `under-voltage`, `protection-system`).
  - Synthesizes the 7-component layout:
    1. **Arduino Uno R3** (`arduino_main`): Positioned at $(100, 220)$.
    2. **Half Breadboard** (`bb_main`): Positioned at $(460, 260)$.
    3. **9V Battery** (`battery_9v`): Positioned at $(840, 120)$.
    4. **Potentiometer** (`pot_input`): Positioned at $(845, 310)$.
    5. **Tactile Pushbutton** (`btn_reset`): Positioned at $(855, 410)$.
    6. **Piezo Buzzer** (`buzzer_alarm`): Positioned at $(915, 390)$.
    7. **16x2 I2C LCD Display** (`lcd_i2c`): Positioned at $(460, 560)$.
  - Synthesizes all 16 netlist connections:
    - `wire_bat_pos`: 9V Battery `+` $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Top `+` Rail [Red].
    - `wire_bat_neg`: 9V Battery `-` $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Top `-` Rail [Black].
    - `wire_pot_vcc`: Breadboard Top `+` Rail $\rightarrow$ Potentiometer Pin 3 [Red].
    - `wire_pot_gnd`: Breadboard Top `-` Rail $\rightarrow$ Potentiometer Pin 1 [Black].
    - `wire_pot_wiper`: Potentiometer Wiper $\rightarrow$ Arduino Pin A0 [Yellow].
    - `wire_aref_jump`: Arduino 3.3V $\rightarrow$ Arduino AREF [Purple jumper on board].
    - `wire_lcd_vcc`: Arduino 5V $\rightarrow$ LCD VCC [Red].
    - `wire_lcd_gnd`: Arduino GND.1 $\rightarrow$ LCD GND [Green].
    - `wire_lcd_sda`: Arduino A4 $\rightarrow$ LCD SDA [Blue].
    - `wire_lcd_scl`: Arduino A5 $\rightarrow$ LCD SCL [Yellow].
    - `wire_buzzer_pos`: Arduino Pin 8 $\rightarrow$ Buzzer `+` [Magenta, looping across top margin and down right side].
    - `wire_buzzer_neg`: Buzzer `-` $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail [Black].
    - `wire_reset_btn`: Arduino RESET $\rightarrow$ Pushbutton Terminal 2a [Orange, looping under LCD].
    - `wire_btn_gnd`: Pushbutton Terminal 1a $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail [Black].
    - `wire_bb_gnd`: Arduino GND.2 $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail [Black].
    - `wire_rail_gnd`: Breadboard Top `-` Rail $\rightarrow$ Breadboard Bottom `-` Rail [Black common ground link].

### 3. SVG Circuit Rendering
- **[js/renderer.js](file:///d:/GitHub/OSS/TCAD-WOKWI-Translator/js/renderer.js)**:
  - Added `getBattery9vSVG()`: 9V battery with positive stud and negative socket snap connectors, copper top band, and 9V branding.
  - Added `getBuzzerSVG()`: Piezo buzzer with dark casing, brass center acoustic hole, and polarity markings.
  - Added `getLcd1602Svg()`: 16x2 LCD display with green PCB frame, dark blue backlit LCD screen, and 4-pin I2C backpack connector (`GND`, `VCC`, `SDA`, `SCL`).
  - Added pin coordinate resolvers in `getPinCoordinates()` for `battery-9v`, `buzzer`, and `lcd1602-i2c`.
  - Added smart curve routing: top overhead sweep for `wire_buzzer_pos`, bottom loop under LCD for `wire_reset_btn`, and on-board vertical arch for `wire_aref_jump`.
